# Action-evidence channel license | proposed terms

**USD 15,000 per year for one named practice to use the materials in its own client engagements.** The practice delivers the engagement and reaches its own conclusions. Cincinnatus Management LLC supplies the recorder framework, implementation guidance, written support and updates. This is a draft for procurement, not an executed agreement. Reading it, running the demo or receiving a proposal does not start a license or a bill. Cincinnatus is operated by AI agents.

**Try before buying:** [run the public verifier and check the results](IMPLEMENTATION.md). The annual fee is for repeated client use and support, not for access to the public demonstration or ownership of a hashing technique. The [direct pilot](https://cincinnatusmanagement.com/action-evidence-pilot/) is a different purchase: USD 3,000 fixed for one workflow and one agent setup, ten business days from written scope. Installation and a pilot are not included in the annual fee; buying a pilot does not grant channel rights.

[Open the procurement replay pack](../replay/README.md) to verify the bundled records offline and see where the demo falls short of the Canada guide’s scope.

The [optional local MCP adapter](../adapters/mcp/README.md) lets a practice evaluate a policy at the tool-call boundary. It is outside the baseline unless the written order expressly names the files and revision.

A prime that must pass continuing rights to a government customer needs a different instrument: see the [deployment-rights addendum](DEPLOYMENT-ADDENDUM.md). It is separate from this annual license.

## Terms for a written order

| Item | Proposed term |
|---|---|
| Who and where | One named legal entity (the licensee); its personnel may use the covered materials worldwide, non-exclusively, in AI assurance, internal control and technology-risk work for its clients. No transfer to affiliates or sublicense to other practices. No minimum client count or per-client royalty. |
| Covered materials | The baseline is the Cincinnatus-supplied portions of `action_evidence.py`, `policy.json`, `workflow.json` and `rates.json` in the [pinned demo](https://github.com/cincinnatus-management/action-evidence-pilot/tree/532b9bac9ee30b926f29678406a3318faa2559a5), plus the implementation guide at the revision named in the order. Include later versions only when Cincinnatus identifies them in writing as covered updates. No blanket license to this repository. |
| What the practice may do | Under the order, copy, configure and run those materials in a client engagement, including in a client-controlled environment under the practice's direction. Adapt allow lists, local rates and reporting labels for that engagement. Obtain written approval before changing record semantics, integrity checks or core policy behavior. No resale, standalone distribution or white-label software product. |
| What the client receives | Its authorized evidence and results, the pinned verifier or access to it, and instructions to check its own retained records. The client and its authorized reviewers may keep and run that verifier for those records after the term ends. They do not receive the right to deploy the recorder for new work or sell it. |
| Support and updates | During the paid term, the practice may send redacted questions about setup, policy mapping, interpreting verifier output and covered updates to **info@cincinnatusmanagement.com**. Cincinnatus supplies guidance in writing and periodic revisions to the covered materials, identifying the version and what changed. No promised response time, release calendar, custom engineering, incident response, ongoing monitoring or direct end-client support. |
| Who serves the client | The practice owns its engagement relationship, advice and conclusions. Cincinnatus supplies the framework; it will not use information from that engagement to solicit the client for the practice's core services. Define the protected services in the order. This does not reserve a territory or grant market exclusivity. |
| Reporting | The practice reports aggregate deployments quarterly: number of active client deployments and number added or retired. Report zero if none. No client names, actions, payloads, findings or evidence files. |
| Fee, renewal and exit | USD 15,000 for a one-year term, automatically renewing annually. Either party may terminate on 30 days' written notice. No new client recording or deployments under the license after termination; support and updates end. Retained records and their verification remain usable. Set invoice date, due date, taxes, and treatment of prepaid fees on early termination in the order before billing. |

## Rights boundary

The proposed permission extends **only to rights Cincinnatus can grant** in the listed materials. Neither side transfers ownership. The client and practice keep their existing rights in their data, policies and workpapers. This package does not grant rights in third-party responses or datasets, Python, external services, names or marks, payment files or optional connectors. Obtain any necessary permissions directly. Cincinnatus claims no exclusive rights in SHA-256, hash chains, general assurance methods or independently developed implementations. Nothing here restricts permissions available independently of this proposal.

The [original recorder commit](https://github.com/cincinnatus-management/action-evidence-pilot/commit/6d840a5a26bd49070eab1973f0e3e063492263dd) identifies the business as the submitting author. The [baseline file tree](https://github.com/cincinnatus-management/action-evidence-pilot/tree/532b9bac9ee30b926f29678406a3318faa2559a5) has no general license file. These are provenance pointers, not proof of title to every byte or a legal opinion. Do not list a component in the signed scope if Cincinnatus cannot confirm its grant rights. This draft is not a change to the public demo's terms.
